# Colorado Block Pros Masonry Design Brief

## Design read
Built for GC estimators, project managers, architects, and public-sector decision makers who need immediate evidence of technical competence, schedule control, and Colorado-specific experience.

## Concept spine
The site is a submittal set made spatial: coursing lines become the grid, specification notes become navigation, and the camera moves from complete structure to the workmanship inside the wall.

## Delivery tier
Cinema. A scroll-controlled single-take film carries the homepage qualification story, supported by restrained page transitions and tactile masonry-derived interaction.

## Locked palette
- Blueprint Black: #111416
- Carbon: #25292B
- Mortar Gray: #C7C7C0
- Plan White: #F3F3EF
- Signal Red: #A61920

The palette comes directly from the existing CBP mark and jobsite documents. Signal Red is the only interface accent; the logo's yellow remains contained within the supplied logo artwork.

## Locked type
Outfit for display and body, paired with IBM Plex Mono for specifications, temperatures, standards, and utility labels. The broad geometric display face echoes block modules without turning the site into a construction cliché.

## Animation mode
Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey
Journey shape: single-shot.

The homepage uses one continuous 15-second film with five semantic chapters:
1. Qualification. A Front Range masonry structure at first light. Headline: Built for the work behind the wall. Copy: Commercial masonry shaped by 25-plus years of field experience, disciplined coordination, and Colorado conditions.
2. Governmental. The camera approaches the building and reveals disciplined wall planes. Headline: Public work without paperwork surprises. Copy: Wage regimes, certified payroll, material documentation, and local labor requirements are treated as project work.
3. Commercial. The move continues along coursing and reinforcement detail. Headline: We plan around inspection. Copy: Mock-ups, strength verification, grout sequencing, and cold-weather procedures are considered before they become schedule problems.
4. Architectural. The lens tracks across architectural CMU and tooled joints. Headline: The finish is the system. Copy: Material compatibility, moisture control, coursing, and color consistency protect the architect's intent.
5. Close. The camera resolves on a clean wall corner in warm Colorado light. Headline: Put a prepared masonry partner on the bid list. Copy: Send the plans, schedule, and project location to start a practical scope conversation.

World grammar: documentary cinematic realism; Colorado Front Range light from camera left; charcoal, concrete gray, and controlled CBP red details; low-saturation material surfaces; crisp joints; no people staring at camera; center-safe structure; dark edge falloff for readable HTML copy; no text, logos, or watermarks in footage.

Mobile framing: every focal masonry detail remains inside the middle 55 percent of the frame. Mobile uses a dedicated 720p encode and the complete story remains readable with motion disabled.

Delivery budget: desktop film at or below 32 MiB; mobile film at or below 16 MiB.

The journey enacts the concept spine by moving from the total project to the exact wall detail, the same way a strong bid moves from qualification into submittals, inspection, and execution.

## Content guardrails
- Technical claims remain edition-aware and avoid unverified EPD, bonding, apprenticeship, project-list, or supplier assertions.
- 25-plus years is framed as supplied company information pending final owner confirmation.
- Visible copy uses no em dashes or en dash separators.
- No generic “quality craftsmanship” filler, invented testimonials, fake metrics, or vague claims.
- The site reads as a qualification document, not a residential lead-generation page.
